85 real male Gold-level player heads

These are cropped Wikimedia Commons photographs. Each image retains its individual licence, creator and source; PNG metadata also carries credit. The edits are head cropping, resizing and a circular alpha edge. No faces were synthesized.

Male/footballer identity is checked through Wikidata and EA's explicit Men's Football field. Gold-level eligibility uses EA overall ≥75, following EA's documented Gold classification. Rating snapshot source. Real-player nationality is only library metadata; fictional generated players keep their independent nationality and names.

This personal project is not endorsed by or affiliated with EA, Wikimedia or the depicted players. Attribution and image licences remain applicable when sharing these image derivatives.
